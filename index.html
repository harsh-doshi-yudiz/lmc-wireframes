These are simple screen sketches of the LMC system. They show what each person will see and do, not the final colours or design. Use the menu on the left, or the Next / Previous buttons at the bottom of every page, to walk through the screens.

How a PNG connection moves through the system

The journey of one house, from the Service (Job) Order in SAP to the final conversion.

SAP

Job Order created

Created in SAP for a work center, and sent to LMC with GA, city and customer details.

GA Admin

Work center mapped

The GA Admin has linked the work center to a contractor's mobile number.

Contractor

Shares houses

Picks GA and City, then gives each house to one Supervisor.

Supervisor

Step 1 · Feasibility

Feasible → assigns a Plumber Team (or Self). Not feasible → house cancelled with a reason.

Plumber Team

Steps 2–5

Plumbing, Ground Connection, Commissioning and Conversion — checklist, tests, photos and GPS.

Supervisor

Approves each step

Approves the step, or sends it back for rework with a remark.

After every completed and approved step

Pushed to SAP The data of that step is sent back to SAP straight away — not only at the end.

If SAP moves the Service Order to another contractor

Contractor changed LMC receives the update from SAP. Approved steps stay with each house; the new contractor's team completes the remaining stages.

When Step 5 is approved — or the house is cancelled

CompletedCancelled→ status is updated for the Contractor and the Admin, and sent to SAP.

If there is a problem at site

Plumber Team raises a Dispute with a reason → Supervisor either marks it Resolved (work continues) or Cancels the connection → Contractor & Admin are notified.

The five steps of every connection

Every house goes through the same five steps, in this order. A step opens only after the previous one is approved.

StepDone byWhat is filled inApproved by
1 · First visit & feasibilitySupervisorFeasible / Not feasible. If not feasible, a standard reason is mandatory and the house is closed as Cancelled. If feasible, the house is assigned to a Plumbing team or to the Supervisor (Self).Pending discussion
2 · PlumbingPlumber TeamJob card: Meter Serial No. and Initial meter reading (read by OCR) · GI plumbing checklist (15 points) · Pipe Pneumatic Testing (PPT) report · HSE complianceSupervisor
3 · Ground Connection (GC)Pending discussionGC checklist (3 points) · GC testing report · EF joint · GC joint no.Supervisor
4 · CommissioningPlumber TeamManometer testing (MMT) checklist (6 points) · rubber tube details (make, mfg. and expiry date) · MMT reportSupervisor
5 · ConversionPlumber TeamNG conversion checklist (2 points) · NG pressure at appliance / burnerSupervisor
On every stepPhoto proof, GPS location (mandatory) and a comments box.
ChecklistsEach inspection point is answered Yes / No with optional remarks. No automatic rules are applied.
After approvalThe step's data is pushed to SAP, and the next step unlocks.
Pending discussionWho approves Step 1 and who performs Step 3 are still being discussed.

Status of a house (lead)

Every house carries one clear status. The same status is shown to everyone — Admin, GA Admin, Contractor, Supervisor and Plumber Team.

Not assigned→Feasibility pending→Feasible→Step 2…5 in progress→Awaiting approval→Completed
Not assigned
House has arrived from SAP (or moved to a new contractor); the Contractor has not yet given it to a Supervisor.
Feasibility pending
Supervisor has the house but Step 1 is not done. It cannot be given to a Plumber Team yet.
Feasible
Step 1 done. Waiting for the Supervisor to assign a Plumber Team (or Self).
Step 3 of 5
Plumber Team is working on this step.
Awaiting approval
A step has been submitted and is waiting for the Supervisor.
Rework
Supervisor sent the step back with a remark. Plumber Team must correct and resubmit.
Disputed
Plumber Team reported a problem at site. Waiting for the Supervisor's decision.
Completed
All five steps approved. Final status sent to SAP.
Cancelled
Marked Not feasible at Step 1, or cancelled by the Supervisor after a dispute — always with a reason.
House locked
House Lock status as received from SAP, shown inside the platform.

How a contractor is linked

The Work Center code is the unique key. The GA Admin adds a contractor's mobile number against one or more work centers in LMC. The Work Center Description is the contractor's name.

Service Order moved to another contractor

SAP sends the update. LMC links the Service Order to the contractor mapped to its new work center. Approved steps stay with each house; the new contractor completes the remaining stages, and the move is logged.
